Alan's Workout

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 July 2026

The short version. Alan's Workout works entirely on your device unless you choose to create an account. There are no ads, no trackers, and no analytics. Nothing is ever sold or shared for advertising. You can export everything you have, and delete your account and all of its data yourself, at any time, from inside the app.

Who we are

Alan's Workout ("the app") is an independently developed workout tracker. For any privacy question, or to exercise any of the rights described below, contact alansworkoutapp@gmail.com.

Using the app without an account

You can use Alan's Workout without signing up. In that case your routines, workout history, personal records and preferences are stored only in your own browser or device storage. They are not transmitted to us, and we cannot see them.

What we collect if you create an account

An account exists so your training history can sync across devices and so you can connect with other people. Creating one means the following is stored on our servers:

  • Account details — your email address, display name and username.
  • Training data — your workout history, sets, repetitions, weights, personal records, routines and training schedule.
  • Body weight — optional. Only used to calculate accurate one-rep-max estimates for bodyweight-assisted exercises such as weighted pull-ups. You never have to provide it, and the app works without it.
  • Preferences — units (kg/lb), theme, accent colour and language.
  • Social activity — who you follow and who follows you, follow requests, the short preset "pings" you send or receive, and any trainer–student connections.

Health information

Body weight and training records can be considered health-related information, so we treat them as sensitive. They are used only to make the app work for you — to display your history and calculate your estimates. They are never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with data brokers.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or renting of personal data, ever.
  • No location tracking, no contacts access, no microphone or camera access.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase — provides the database, authentication and hosting that store your account and synced training data.
  • Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google" to sign in. In that case Google confirms your identity to us; we receive your email address and name. If you sign up with an email and password instead, Google is not involved.
  • GitHub Pages — serves the app itself, and like any web server processes your IP address in order to deliver the page.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed from our database immediately, along with everything attached to it. Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

Your rights

  • Access and portability — Settings → Data → Export Backup downloads everything the app holds as a JSON file, at any time, without asking us.
  • Deletion — Settings → Account → Delete account permanently removes your account and its data. See the account deletion page for details.
  • Correction — you can edit your name, username and training data directly in the app.
  • Questions or complaints — email alansworkoutapp@gmail.com. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Alan's Workout is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
