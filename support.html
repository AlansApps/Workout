Support

We read everything that comes in.

Email alansworkoutapp@gmail.com for anything at all — a bug, a question, a feature idea, a privacy request, or to report another user. We aim to reply within a few days.

Reporting abuse or objectionable content

If someone is behaving abusively, or you see content that shouldn't be there, email us with the username involved and a short description of what happened. Reports are acted on promptly, and accounts that breach the Terms of Use can be suspended or removed.

Getting your data out

You don't need to ask us. In the app, go to Settings → Data → Export Backup to download everything the app holds about you as a JSON file.

Deleting your account

Also self-service: Settings → Account → Delete account. Full details, including exactly what gets removed, are on the account deletion page.

Reporting a bug

The more of this you can include, the faster it gets fixed:

  • What you were doing when it happened.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Your device and browser, or how you installed the app.
  • A screenshot, if you have one.

Before you email about lost data

If you've been using the app without an account, your training history lives only on that device and in that browser. Clearing your browser data, or switching to a different browser or phone, means it won't be there. Creating an account keeps it synced and safe across devices, and Export Backup gives you your own copy either way.
