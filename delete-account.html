Delete Your Account

Self-service, no need to ask us.

You can do this yourself, in the app, in under a minute. This page exists so the instructions are reachable even if you have already removed the app from your device.

In the app

  1. Open Alan's Workout and go to your Profile.
  2. Tap the gear icon in the top right to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Type your username to confirm, then tap Delete forever.

Want a copy of your training history first? Use Settings → Data → Export Backup before you delete — it downloads everything as a JSON file.

If you no longer have the app

Email alansworkoutapp@gmail.com from the address your account is registered to, asking for deletion. We will confirm and delete the account. Requests are handled within 30 days, normally much sooner.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, from our servers:

  • Your profile, username, display name and email address
  • Your entire workout history — every session, exercise and set
  • Your personal records and estimated one-rep-max figures
  • Your routines and training rotation
  • Your body weight and all preferences
  • Followers, people you follow, and pending follow requests
  • Pings you have sent or received
  • Any trainer–student connections and assigned routines
  • Any badges or insignias you have earned

This is immediate and permanent. There is no recovery period and no way to undo it.

What stays on your own device

Workout history saved locally on the device you were using is not touched, so you can keep training as a guest straight afterwards. To remove that too, clear the site data for Alan's Workout in your browser settings, or delete the app from your home screen.
